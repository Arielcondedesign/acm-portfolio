# People of Leisure

## Sustainable fashion e-commerce redesign (Shopify)

**Role:** UX/UI Designer (freelance) · **Team:** Client's marketing and development · **Platform:** Shopify responsive

---

### The challenge

People of Leisure is a sustainable fashion brand based in Los Angeles. The brand needed to rejuvenate its digital identity to connect with a younger, more conscious audience aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals, without losing its existing customer base. The challenge was twofold: modernise the Shopify shopping experience and articulate a sustainability narrative that was credible and contemporary — not a generic claim.

---

### UX Research: cross-data and real validation

#### Data-driven segmentation

The research started with a **cross-analysis of Google Analytics and Shopify Analytics**. By combining browsing behaviour data with purchase metrics, we defined two main profiles: an audience with greater purchasing power interested in sustainable fashion as an investment, and younger consumers looking for purpose-driven brands without compromising on design. This segmentation was the foundation of all subsequent design decisions.

#### Tree testing

To validate the menu reorganisation and new category structure, I ran a **tree test** with representative users from both profiles. The test revealed that the original navigation scattered user attention across too many categories with little product, causing frustration and early drop-offs. The results directly guided the menu simplification and the grouping of categories by garment type.

#### Iterative validation with stakeholders

Prototypes developed in Figma were validated with the brand's CEO through **remote iterative sessions**. This agile format allowed key decisions to be made before development, avoiding rework and ensuring that the founder's vision — deeply committed to sustainability — was reflected in every screen without compromising usability.

#### Validated sustainability narrative

In parallel with the interface design, a **strategic narrative around the brand's sustainable practices** was developed. This narrative incorporated references to the SDGs and CO₂ offsets on shipments, and was tested with users to ensure the impact communication was perceived as authentic and not as greenwashing.

---

### Design process

#### Shopify template redesign

The intervention included a complete redesign of the Shopify template: new information architecture, deep reorganisation of the catalogue and conversion optimisation through navigation consistent with the real behaviour we had observed in the data.

#### Focus on product and reduced scatter

One of the most impactful changes was simplifying the visible offer. Instead of showing the entire catalogue in a flat structure, best-performing products were prioritised and clearer paths to the key categories were created. This not only improved the browsing experience but concentrated conversion on the most profitable products.

#### Sustainability integrated into the UX

A plugin was integrated that planted a tree for every purchase made. This element wasn't just a gesture of social responsibility: it was designed as a **positive reinforcement moment** in the purchase flow, right after payment — consolidating customer loyalty with a tangible, shareable action.

---

### Strategic approach: the full funnel

- **Acquisition:** Improved content hierarchy and information architecture for organic positioning.
- **Activation:** A younger, fresher narrative validated in prototypes before development. Simplified navigation reducing clicks to product.
- **Retention:** Positive impact plugin (one tree per purchase) as an emotional loyalty mechanism.
- **Revenue:** Optimised catalogue focused on best-performing products, reducing scatter.
- **Referral:** Sustainable repositioning that opened the door to social media amplification and partnerships with aligned brands.

---

### Results

- Complete restructuring of the Shopify store with a new navigation architecture.
- Prototypes validated with the client in Figma.
- Sustainability plugin integrated and aligned with the brand's value proposition.
- Rejuvenated brand image with a coherent, contemporary and digitally native narrative.

---

### What I learned

The most valuable lesson from this project was that **positive impact communication only builds trust when delivered through a contemporary, digitally native language**. A committed founder is a huge asset, but if the digital channel doesn't translate that commitment into the user experience with the same level of care, the proposition loses credibility. It also confirmed the value of tree testing as a fast and effective tool for validating information architecture decisions before touching a single line of code.
